Current problem

  • Lack of follow-up

  • Incomplete information for support

Benefits

Each CampusLink feature improves something concrete for the person reporting and the person resolving

  • 01

    Faster reporting

    Report from a QR code and a short form without wasting time finding who to notify

    Result: Less friction for students and teachers

  • 02

    More precise location

    Identify the affected room or area; support receives the exact problem location

    Result: Less confusion and fewer unnecessary trips

  • 03

    Clear evidence

    Photo and short description to understand the issue before arriving

    Result: Better diagnosis and more prepared service

  • 04

    Transparent tracking

    Visible report statuses: pending, in progress, and resolved

    Result: Less uncertainty and greater trust

  • 05

    Operational prioritization

    Organize reports by criticality, category, status, or location

    Result: More efficient handling of important cases

Who it is for

CampusLink is designed for those who report incidents and for those who handle them on campus.

  • Students

    People who use campus spaces every day.

    Problem

    They detect issues in classrooms, restrooms, labs, or common areas, but they do not always have a clear channel to report them.

    Action

    They report incidents with a QR code, photo, and short description.

    Value

    They know their report does not disappear and can check its progress.

  • Teachers

    People who teach classes and depend on rooms and equipment.

    Problem

    A technical or infrastructure issue can interrupt the development of a class.

    Action

    They request support when a room or piece of equipment affects academic continuity.

    Value

    They reduce lost time and keep greater control during the session.

  • Support and operations staff

    People who receive and resolve reports.

    Problem

    They receive incomplete, ambiguous reports or reports without an exact location.

    Action

    They see the report location, category, evidence, and status.

    Value

    They prioritize better and avoid unnecessary trips.
